# Anti-Slop Audit 001 — Customer Profile & Device Notifications (2026-11-20)

> **Design Read:** Reading this as: `Customer Profile & Device Notification Pipeline` for `scheduled FTL logistics customers & drivers`, in a `Tanaw unified light brand (#002D54 Deep Navy / #0383D5 Electric Blue / Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter + JetBrains Mono)` style, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`.

## Findings List

1. **[HIGH] R-26 / C-2 (Interactive Elements & Functional Completeness) — `CustomerProfileHeader` (`CustomerProfileComponents.kt`)**
   - **Reason:** The camera overlay button on the customer avatar opened an informational stub dialog instead of allowing real camera capture or gallery photo selection.
   - **Approved Fix:** Replace the stub dialog with a functional social-media-style Profile Photo Bottom Sheet supporting **Take Photo** (Camera via `FileProvider`), **Choose from Gallery** (Photo Picker), and **Remove Photo**, with center-square bitmap downsampling (`256x256` JPEG), local persistence (`CustomerAvatarStore`), and Supabase `public.users.avatar_url` synchronization.

2. **[HIGH] R-38 / C-3 (Real Content & Legal Completeness) — `CustomerSubPanel` (`CustomerProfileComponents.kt`)**
   - **Reason:** Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Help & Support sub-panels rendered empty "not published" notices when `system_settings` lacked external URLs, leaving customers without readable terms, privacy disclosures, or company policies.
   - **Approved Fix:** Populate full, structured in-app **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Company & FTL Policies / Help & Support** directly inside `CustomerSubPanel`, while retaining optional external browser links when configured.

3. **[MEDIUM] R-04 / R-31 (Icon Relevance & Visual Consistency) — `CustomerMenuCard` (`CustomerProfileComponents.kt`)**
   - **Reason:** Menu items mixed a raster PNG (`ic_shield_lock.png`), a non-square filled vector (`ic_nav_book.xml`), a solid filled pin (`ic_location_pin.xml`), a misleading status checkmark (`ic_check_circle.xml` for Terms of Service), and Material Outlined vectors (`PrivacyTip`, `HelpOutline`).
   - **Approved Fix:** Unify all `CustomerMenuCard` and `CustomerMenuItemRow` icons onto crisp Material Outlined `ImageVector`s (`LocalShipping`, `LocationOn`, `Lock`, `Description`, `PrivacyTip`, `HelpOutline`, and `ChevronRight`) under an `Account & Policies` section header.

4. **[MEDIUM] R-04 / R-31 (Section vs. Row Icon Redundancy) — `CustomerPersonalInfoCard` & `CustomerSmsCard` (`CustomerProfileComponents.kt`)**
   - **Reason:** `Icons.Outlined.Person` was duplicated in both the `Personal Information` header and `Full Name` row; `Icons.Outlined.Notifications` was duplicated in both the `SMS Notifications` header and toggle card row; `Default Address` used a solid filled drawable instead of an outlined vector.
   - **Approved Fix:** Use `Icons.Outlined.Badge` for the `Personal Information` section header, `Icons.Outlined.LocationOn` for `Default Address`, and `Icons.Outlined.Sms` for the `Shipment SMS Alerts` toggle card row.

5. **[LOW] R-04 / R-39 (Action Affordances in Sub-Panel) — `CustomerSubPanel` (`CustomerProfileComponents.kt`)**
   - **Reason:** Published office contact actions in `Help & Support` lacked leading semantic icons (`Email`, `Phone`, `OpenInNew`).
   - **Approved Fix:** Add leading `Icons.Outlined.Email`, `Icons.Outlined.Phone`, and `Icons.AutoMirrored.Outlined.OpenInNew` icons to contact action rows.
